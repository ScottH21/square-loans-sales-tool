How to apply for a Square Loan

1
Check your offer in Square Dashboard
Sign in and go to Banking › Loans. If you have an offer, it's waiting there. Square reviews your account daily — if nothing's showing yet, keep processing and we'll notify you when one arrives.
Offers are by invitation — no offer means not yet eligible
2
Choose your loan amount
Use the slider to pick the amount that fits — from $1,000 up to your maximum offer. The fee and daily repayment rate adjust as you move the slider. A lower amount means a lower repayment rate.
You don't have to take the full offer. A smaller loan means a smaller daily repayment — useful if you want more cash flowing day to day.
3
Complete your application
Fill in your personal and business details for identity verification — legal name, date of birth, SSN or ITIN, and business EIN if applicable. Usually takes just a few minutes.
LLC, S Corp, C Corp, or Partnership? You'll also need ownership details for anyone owning 25% or more of the business.
4
Review and accept your terms
Before submitting you'll see the full breakdown — loan amount, flat fee, total repayment, and daily repayment %. Review everything, then select I accept. Applications are typically reviewed within 1–2 business days.
Applying has no impact on your personal credit score. Square does not report to third-party credit bureaus.
5
Receive your funds
Once approved, funds arrive in your linked bank account within 1–3 business days. With Square Checking, the money arrives instantly — spend it right away with your Square Debit Card.
Instant funding available with Square Checking
6
Repayments happen automatically
A fixed percentage of your daily card sales is deducted each day — nothing to set up or remember. Repayments flex with your volume, always in proportion to what you're bringing in.
Once your first loan is fully repaid, you may become eligible for a new offer. Check your Dashboard to see what's available.
